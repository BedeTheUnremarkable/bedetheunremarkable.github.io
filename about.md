# About

I’m Bede The Unremarkable. This site is a place to collect my writing and creative projects and to give them a home of their own. I’m a young adult male English undergraduate student living in the United States with an interest in other forms of art, like illustrations and comics. I have a lot to say, from essays on serious topics to fiction and musings on media and shit nobody cares about. Furthermore, I’m excited to share all this with you if you’re willing to give me some of your time.
